Program Overview

Support your heart with consistent food choices

Heart health is shaped by long-term patterns, not one meal. A heart-conscious eating pattern can include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, pulses, nuts and seeds, with attention to the type and amount of fat and salt in your diet.

Your plan is adapted to your medical history, medications, food preferences and family routine. It is designed to work alongside the advice of your treating doctor.

Heart-conscious eating is not about removing enjoyment. It is about making thoughtful choices more often.

04

What is included

What your plan may include

Your exact plan is shaped after a conversation about your needs. The guidance may include the following, adapted to your situation.

  • A review of your usual meals, cooking style and food preferences
  • Guidance on vegetables, fruits, whole grains, pulses and fibre-rich foods
  • Practical ways to choose and portion fats and oils
  • Ideas to reduce excess sodium without losing flavour
  • Meal ideas for home cooking, celebrations and eating out
  • Regular review and coordination with your medical care
