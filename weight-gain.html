Program Overview

Gain weight without leaving nutrition behind

Healthy weight gain is about more than adding extra calories. It means meeting your body's energy and protein needs with a balanced pattern of meals and snacks that you can follow consistently.

Your plan is built around your appetite, food preferences, medical history and daily routine. We focus on nutrient-dense choices, comfortable meal timing and gradual progress rather than quick fixes.

The goal is steady progress with meals that feel satisfying, familiar and sustainable.

Discuss This Program
Who it is for

Who this program can support

Nutrition support works best when it reflects your starting point. These are some of the situations this program is designed for.

01

Low body weight or underweight

If your current weight is below a comfortable range and you want structured nutrition support.

02

Poor appetite or low intake

When meals feel difficult or portions are consistently smaller than your needs.

03

Recovery and rebuilding

Support after illness or a period of unintentional weight loss, alongside medical advice.

04

Active people with higher needs

For those who need more energy and protein to support training and daily activity.

What is included

What your plan may include

Your exact plan is shaped after a conversation about your needs. The guidance may include the following, adapted to your situation.

  • An initial review of your food pattern, appetite, routine and health goals
  • Guidance on energy-dense foods, protein sources and balanced meal combinations
  • Practical meal and snack ideas built around your preferences
  • Suggestions for grocery planning, portions and preparation
  • Regular review and adjustments as your appetite and needs change
  • Coordination with your treating doctor where a medical condition is involved
How it works

A simple, supported way forward

No complicated rules. We start with understanding, build a practical plan and keep refining it with you.
