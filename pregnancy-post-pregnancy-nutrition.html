Program Overview

Nourishment that changes with each stage

Nutrition needs shift through pregnancy, the postpartum period and breastfeeding. A practical plan should support your energy, recovery and overall wellbeing while respecting food safety and the advice of your maternity care team.

We build guidance around your current routine, appetite, food preferences and any medical considerations. Supplements and specific restrictions are handled in line with your doctor's recommendations.

Nutrition support should meet you where you are, from the first trimester through recovery and breastfeeding.

Discuss This Program
Who it is for

Who this program can support

Nutrition support works best when it reflects your starting point. These are some of the situations this program is designed for.

01

Preparing for pregnancy

A practical review of everyday nutrition before conception, alongside medical advice.

02

During pregnancy

Support with balanced meals, appetite changes and food safety at each stage.

03

Postpartum recovery

Nourishing food ideas for recovery when time and energy may feel limited.

04

Breastfeeding

Guidance on meals and hydration that fit around feeding and your daily routine.

What is included

What your plan may include

Your exact plan is shaped after a conversation about your needs. The guidance may include the following, adapted to your situation.

  • A review of your current meals, appetite and daily routine
  • Guidance on balanced meals, protein, iron-rich foods and fibre
  • Practical food-safety considerations for pregnancy
  • Ideas for comfortable meals during nausea, low appetite or fatigue
  • Postpartum and breastfeeding nutrition guidance
  • Coordination with your treating doctor or maternity care team
How it works

A simple, supported way forward

No complicated rules. We start with understanding, build a practical plan and keep refining it with you.

Step 01

Understand your starting point

We begin with your goals, food preferences, daily routine and relevant health information.

Step 02

Build a practical plan

Your nutrition guidance is shaped around food you enjoy and changes you can realistically maintain.

Step 03

Review and adjust

As your needs and routine change, we refine the approach with you over time.
